# Inside iPlant section redesign

The previous eight-image mosaic was removed because its uneven cards, mixed ratios and drifting tiles made the section feel visually restless and disconnected.

The replacement is a focused field journal:

- one large, consistent image stage
- five clear steps: test, control, grow, harvest and deploy
- simple text navigation without boxed cards
- crossfade and subtle scale transitions between real photographs
- aligned edges and one stable 16:10 image ratio
- reduced homepage height
- matching English and Arabic content
- keyboard, hover and touch interaction

No other approved homepage structure was changed.
